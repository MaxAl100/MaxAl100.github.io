Celeste modding: MaxAl Helper

C# helper library and 13 published mods for the Everest mod loader
C# Everest Lönn plugins Open source

MaxAl Helper is my open-source code mod for Celeste, built on the Everest mod loader. It includes Lönn editor entities and custom particle graphics so level makers can use its content directly in their maps. Alongside it I publish my own mods on GameBanana, where I have built levels around specific ideas and managed teams of mappers to achieve larger projects that any of them could have achieved on their own.

What it shows
Extending an existing game through its modding framework in C#, and shipping content other players download.
Scale
39 commits in the repository; 13 mods published on GameBanana.
